Unit 6 Review

Non-permissible values, simplifying, the four operations, rational equations and their problems on one page, then mixed examples that combine them. One question runs the whole unit: is there an equals sign? An expression keeps its denominator and lists its NPVs; an equation is multiplied by the LCD to clear its denominators, and every root is checked against the NPVs.

I can…
  • state the non-permissible values from the original expression, including a divisor’s numerator
  • simplify, multiply and divide rational expressions by dividing out common factors (never terms)
  • add and subtract rational expressions over the LCD, with brackets when subtracting
  • solve rational equations, reject extraneous roots and verify
  • solve motion and work problems with a table, rejecting inadmissible roots

Simplest form checklist
1Every numerator and denominator factored fully (GCF first, then trinomial or difference of squares).
2No factor common to the numerator and the denominator (look for opposites like 3-x and x-3).
3Like denominators combined into one fraction.
4NPVs stated from the original expression, including any divisor’s numerator.

The simplified expression is equivalent to the original for every permissible value.

Rational equations. An equation is solved when the variable is out of the denominators: multiply every term (including terms with no denominator) by the LCD. A root that is a non-permissible value would make a denominator 0, so it is extraneous; if every root is extraneous, there is no solution.

Common errors tap a card to see the fix

\dfrac{x+6}{x+3}\ne2
The x’s are terms, not factors: nothing divides out.
NPVs taken from the simplified answer
They miss the cancelled factors: take NPVs from the original.
\dfrac{a}{b}-\dfrac{c+d}{b}
=\dfrac{a-c-d}{b}, not \dfrac{a-c+d}{b}
Forgetting to multiply a constant term by the LCD
Multiply every term, including terms with no denominator.

Expressions

Factor first, always. The NPVs come from the original denominators before anything divides out, and only factors divide out. The answer is a fraction with its NPVs beside it.
Example 1

NPVs and simplifying

State the non-permissible values, then simplify.

(a) \dfrac{5x}{2x^2+7x-4} (NPVs only)
2x^2+7x-4=(2x-1)(x+4)
\boldsymbol{x\ne\tfrac{1}{2},\ -4} (it is already in simplest form)
(b) \dfrac{3x^2-12x}{x^2-x-12}
=\dfrac{3x(x-4)}{(x-4)(x+3)}=\boldsymbol{\dfrac{3x}{x+3}}, \boldsymbol{x\ne4,\ -3}
(c) \dfrac{10-5x}{x^2-4}
=\dfrac{-5(x-2)}{(x-2)(x+2)}=\boldsymbol{-\dfrac{5}{x+2}}, \boldsymbol{x\ne\pm2}
(d) \dfrac{x^2-9y^2}{x^2+xy-6y^2} (NPVs in terms of y)
=\dfrac{(x-3y)(x+3y)}{(x+3y)(x-2y)}=\boldsymbol{\dfrac{x-3y}{x-2y}}, \boldsymbol{x\ne-3y,\ 2y}
Example 2

Multiplying and dividing

Simplify and state the non-permissible values.

(a) \dfrac{x^2-25}{4x+8}\cdot\dfrac{x^2+2x}{x^2-3x-10}
=\dfrac{(x-5)(x+5)}{4(x+2)}\cdot\dfrac{x(x+2)}{(x-5)(x+2)}=\boldsymbol{\dfrac{x(x+5)}{4(x+2)}}, \boldsymbol{x\ne-2,\ 5}
(b) \dfrac{2x^2+5x-3}{x^2-9}\div\dfrac{4x^2-1}{x^2-3x}
=\dfrac{(2x-1)(x+3)}{(x-3)(x+3)}\cdot\dfrac{x(x-3)}{(2x-1)(2x+1)}=\boldsymbol{\dfrac{x}{2x+1}}
NPVs: x\ne\pm3 (first denominator), x\ne0,\ 3 (second denominator), x\ne\pm\frac{1}{2} (the divisor’s numerator). So \boldsymbol{x\ne-3,\ 0,\ 3,\ -\frac{1}{2},\ \frac{1}{2}}.

Every denominator, even after the flip

Dividing is where NPVs go missing. Collect them before anything divides out: the zeros of every original denominator, then the zeros of the divisor’s numerator, which becomes a denominator when the divisor flips.

Example 3

Adding and subtracting

Simplify and state the non-permissible values.

(a) \dfrac{5}{6x^2}-\dfrac{3}{4x}+\dfrac{1}{3}
LCD =12x^2, x\ne0
=\dfrac{10}{12x^2}-\dfrac{9x}{12x^2}+\dfrac{4x^2}{12x^2}
=\boldsymbol{\dfrac{4x^2-9x+10}{12x^2}}, \boldsymbol{x\ne0}
(b) \dfrac{2x}{x^2-4}-\dfrac{1}{x-2}
LCD =(x-2)(x+2), x\ne\pm2
=\dfrac{2x-(x+2)}{(x-2)(x+2)}=\dfrac{x-2}{(x-2)(x+2)}
=\boldsymbol{\dfrac{1}{x+2}}, \boldsymbol{x\ne\pm2}
Example 4

Mixed operations and a rectangle

(a) Simplify \left(\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\div\dfrac{x+2}{x^2} and state the non-permissible values.
=\dfrac{2+x}{2x}\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{x+2}=\boldsymbol{\dfrac{x}{2}}, \boldsymbol{x\ne0} (denominators) and \boldsymbol{x\ne-2} (the divisor’s numerator)
(b) A rectangle has area \dfrac{3x^2+10x-8}{x^2-4} and length \dfrac{3x-2}{x-2}. Write its width in simplest form, then find the length and width when x=6.
Width = area \div length =\dfrac{(3x-2)(x+4)}{(x-2)(x+2)}\cdot\dfrac{x-2}{3x-2}=\boldsymbol{\dfrac{x+4}{x+2}}, x\ne\pm2,\ \frac{2}{3}
x=6: length =\frac{16}{4}=\boldsymbol{4}, width =\frac{10}{8}=\boldsymbol{\frac{5}{4}}. (Check: area =\frac{160}{32}=5=4\times\frac{5}{4} ✓)

Equations and Problems

Five steps. 1 State the NPVs. 2 Multiply every term by the LCD. 3 Solve (linear or quadratic). 4 Reject any root that is an NPV (extraneous). 5 Verify in the original equation.
Example 5

Solve and verify

State the non-permissible values, solve and verify.

(a) \dfrac{3}{x+2}=\dfrac{5}{2x-1}
x\ne-2,\ \frac{1}{2}
3(2x-1)=5(x+2)
6x-3=5x+10
\boldsymbol{x=13}
Verify: \frac{3}{15}=\frac{1}{5} and \frac{5}{25}=\frac{1}{5} ✓
(b) \dfrac{x}{x-3}-\dfrac{18}{x^2-9}=\dfrac{4}{x+3}
x\ne\pm3; LCD (x-3)(x+3)
x(x+3)-18=4(x-3)
x^2+3x-18=4x-12\Rightarrow x^2-x-6=0
(x-3)(x+2)=0
x=3 is an NPV (extraneous); \boldsymbol{x=-2}
Verify: \frac{-2}{-5}-\frac{18}{-5}=4 and \frac{4}{1}=4 ✓
Verify on the calculator. Store the root in X: (−)2STO→X,T,θ,nENTER. Then type each side with X,T,θ,n for x and brackets around every denominator: the left side gives 4 and the right side gives 4 ✓.
Example 6

No solution, and why it happens

(a) Show that \dfrac{2x}{x-4}=3+\dfrac{8}{x-4} has no solution.
x\ne4. Multiply by x-4: 2x=3(x-4)+8\Rightarrow2x=3x-4\Rightarrow x=4.
The only root is the non-permissible value, so it is extraneous: the equation has no solution.
(b) Explain why a root of the equation you get after multiplying by the LCD may not be a root of the original equation.
Multiplying by the LCD removes the denominators, so the new equation is defined even where the original is not. A root that makes the LCD 0 satisfies the new equation but makes the original undefined (division by 0).

Keep it or reject it? each side of the equation is a graph

A solution is an x where the graphs of the left side and the right side meet. An extraneous root is a place where they don’t.

Problems: the table first

Put what you know in a table, write the unknown in each row, and let the sentence that compares them give the equation: same time, so many hours longer, or for a job, the parts done add up to 1. A negative speed or time is inadmissible.

Example 7

The train and the bus

A train travels 540 km. A bus that is 30 km/h slower takes 1.5 h longer to cover the same distance. Determine both speeds and both times.

Train s, bus s-30: \dfrac{540}{s-30}-\dfrac{540}{s}=\dfrac{3}{2}, s\ne0,\ 30.
Multiply by 2s(s-30): 1080s-1080(s-30)=3s(s-30)\Rightarrow32\,400=3s^2-90s\Rightarrow s^2-30s-10\,800=0
(s-120)(s+90)=0; s=120 (-90 rejected)
Train 120 km/h for 4.5 h; bus 90 km/h for 6 h.
Example 8

Two snowplows

Working alone, one snowplow takes 4 h longer than another to clear a parking lot. Together they clear it in 4 h 48 min. How long does each take alone?

4 h 48 min =4.8 h. Faster x, slower x+4: \dfrac{4.8}{x}+\dfrac{4.8}{x+4}=1, x\ne0,\ -4.
4.8(x+4)+4.8x=x(x+4)\Rightarrow x^2-5.6x-19.2=0\Rightarrow5x^2-28x-96=0\Rightarrow(x-8)(5x+12)=0
x=8 (-\frac{12}{5} rejected). The plows take 8 h and 12 h. Check: 0.6+0.4=1 ✓

Key ideas

NPVs. Factor every denominator of the original expression; each zero is an NPV. When dividing, the divisor’s numerator gives NPVs too. NPVs go beside every answer.
Simplify, multiply, divide. Factor everything, divide out common factors (never terms; \frac{a-b}{b-a}=-1), and multiply by the reciprocal to divide.
Add and subtract. Factor the denominators, build the LCD, rewrite each fraction over it, combine the numerators with brackets when subtracting, then simplify.
Equations and problems. NPVs, multiply every term by the LCD, solve, reject extraneous roots (NPVs) and inadmissible ones (negative speeds or times), verify.
